BodyScale 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-22

  • 数据存在本机：你的资料（名字、身高、生日、性别）和测量记录只保存在你自己的手机上，我们没有服务器，也收不到这些数据。
  • 蓝牙：只用于连接体脂秤读取测量数据。
  • 苹果健康（可选）：只有在你允许后，App 才把体重、体脂率、去脂体重和 BMI 写入苹果健康；App 不读取你的健康数据。你可以随时在“健康”App 里关闭权限。
  • 自定义 Webhook（可选，默认关闭）：只有你自己填写了网址和令牌，App 才会通过 HTTPS 把测量结果发到你指定的地址。令牌保存在手机的钥匙串里。
  • 不追踪、不投放广告、不使用第三方统计或分析工具，不出售或分享你的数据。
  • 删除数据：删除 App 即可清除本机全部数据。

BodyScale Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-22

  • On-device storage: your profile (name, height, birthday, sex) and measurements are stored only on your phone. We run no server and receive none of this data.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect to the scale and read measurements.
  • Apple Health (optional): only with your permission, the app writes weight, body fat %, lean body mass and BMI to Apple Health. It does not read your health data. You can revoke access anytime in the Health app.
  • Custom webhook (optional, off by default): only if you enter an address and token yourself, the app sends measurements over HTTPS to that address. The token is kept in the device Keychain.
  • No tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics; we never sell or share your data.
  • Deleting data: deleting the app removes all its data from your phone.
